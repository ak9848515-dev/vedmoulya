**Version:** 1.0
**Status:** Draft
**Owner:** Chief Technology Officer
**Created:** 2026-07-24
**Updated:** 2026-07-24

## Purpose

Provide a secure, reliable, and compliant payment infrastructure that enables all financial transactions within the VedMoulya ecosystem.

## Scope

- Payment processing (cards, wallets, bank transfers)
- Subscription and recurring billing management
- Invoice generation and payment collection
- Payouts and disbursements to users
- Currency conversion and multi-currency support
- Tax calculation and reporting
- Refund and chargeback management
- Financial reconciliation and reporting

## Responsibilities

- Process payments securely with PCI compliance
- Integrate with payment gateways and processors
- Manage subscription lifecycles and billing events
- Handle payout scheduling to service providers
- Generate financial reports for users and admins
- Ensure transaction audit trails and compliance
