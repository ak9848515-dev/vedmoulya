## Purpose

Support business operations including user-generated ventures, service offerings, client management, and financial tracking for users building livelihoods on the platform.

## Scope

- Business profile and entity management
- Service offering catalog management
- Client relationship management (CRM)
- Invoice and billing management
- Business analytics and reporting
- Compliance documentation tracking

## Responsibilities

- Enable users to create and manage business profiles
- Support service listing and discovery
- Facilitate client communication and contracts
- Track business financial health and growth metrics
- Generate business performance reports
