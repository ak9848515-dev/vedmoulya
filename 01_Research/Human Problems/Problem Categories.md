## Description

Defines the 15 problem categories that organize all human problems in the VedMoulya Human Problems Repository. Every problem must be assigned to at least one primary category.

---

## Category Definitions

### 1. Career

Problems related to career discovery, planning, advancement, transitions, and professional fulfillment.

_Examples: Choosing a career path, career stagnation, job transitions, imposter syndrome, workplace discrimination._

### 2. Learning

Problems related to acquiring knowledge and skills effectively, efficiently, and sustainably.

_Examples: Information overload, forgetting what you learned, lack of motivation, no personalized learning paths, skill gaps._

### 3. Freelancing

Problems specific to independent work, gig economy participation, and self-employment.

_Examples: Inconsistent income, client acquisition difficulty, scope creep, isolation, lack of benefits._

### 4. Business

Problems related to starting, running, scaling, and sustaining a business.

_Examples: Validating business ideas, cash flow management, hiring, scaling operations, competition._

### 5. Finance

Problems related to personal and business financial management, planning, and security.

_Examples: Budgeting, saving, investing, debt management, financial literacy, retirement planning._

### 6. Execution

Problems related to turning intentions into actions and completing goals reliably.

_Examples: Procrastination, lack of discipline, inconsistency, failing to follow through, overwhelm._

### 7. Productivity

Problems related to managing time, energy, and focus to maximize output.

_Examples: Time management, distraction, prioritization, burnout, low energy, task switching._

### 8. AI Adoption

Problems related to understanding, adopting, and leveraging AI tools effectively.

_Examples: AI anxiety, knowing which tools to use, prompt engineering, AI integration into workflow, fear of obsolescence._

### 9. Communication

Problems related to expressing ideas clearly, persuasively, and effectively across contexts.

_Examples: Public speaking, writing, negotiation, difficult conversations, cross-cultural communication._

### 10. Leadership

Problems related to leading teams, influencing others, and building organizational capability.

_Examples: Delegation, team motivation, decision-making under uncertainty, conflict resolution, vision setting._

### 11. Networking

Problems related to building and maintaining professional relationships and social capital.

_Examples: Approaching strangers, maintaining connections, networking fatigue, building trust, finding mentors._

### 12. Marketing

Problems related to promoting products, services, or personal brands to target audiences.

_Examples: Audience building, content creation, branding, messaging, channel selection, measuring ROI._

### 13. Sales

Problems related to convincing prospects to purchase products or services.

_Examples: Prospecting, objection handling, closing, pricing conversations, follow-up, sales systems._

### 14. Mindset

Problems related to beliefs, attitudes, and mental frameworks that enable or block success.

_Examples: Fixed mindset, fear of failure, self-doubt, scarcity mindset, resistance to change, perfectionism._

### 15. Personal Growth

Problems related to self-improvement beyond career — health, relationships, purpose, and fulfillment.

_Examples: Work-life balance, finding purpose, building habits, emotional intelligence, resilience._

---

## Cross-Categorization

Problems may belong to multiple categories. When cross-categorizing:

1. **Primary category** — The single most relevant category (required)
2. **Secondary categories** — Any additional categories that apply (optional, up to 3)
3. **Cross-reference** — Link to related problems in other categories

---

_New categories can be proposed during quarterly repository reviews. Approval required from Principal Research Architect._
