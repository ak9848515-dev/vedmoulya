## Observation Best Practices

1. **Be a fly on the wall** — Minimize your presence and influence
2. **Take raw notes first** — Separate observation from interpretation; note interpretation separately
3. **Capture context** — Environment, tools, time pressure, and emotional state matter
4. **Look for workarounds** — These reveal unmet needs
5. **Note what's NOT happening** — Missing behaviors are as informative as observed ones
6. **Record timing** — Task duration reveals complexity and friction
7. **Ask permission** — Always get consent before observing
8. **Debrief after** — Review notes immediately while details are fresh

---

_Observation complements interviews by revealing what users do, not just what they say._
