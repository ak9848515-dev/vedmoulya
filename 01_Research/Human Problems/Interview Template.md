## Interview Best Practices

1. **Listen more than you speak** — Aim for 80% participant talking time
2. **Ask "why" repeatedly** — Go five levels deep on each pain point
3. **Avoid leading questions** — "Tell me about..." instead of "Don't you think that..."
4. **Capture verbatim quotes** — Don't paraphrase; exact words matter
5. **Note emotional cues** — How someone says something is as important as what they say
6. **Debrief immediately** — Write up notes within 2 hours of the interview
7. **Respect time** — Stick to the scheduled duration
8. **Thank participants** — Always express gratitude for their time and honesty
