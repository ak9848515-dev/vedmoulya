## Description

Defines the phased roadmap for building the VedMoulya Human Problems Repository from initial foundation to comprehensive knowledge base.

---

## Guiding Principles

1. **Quality over quantity** — Each problem must be validated before it counts toward phase completion
2. **Diversity of categories** — Distribute research across categories, not concentrated in one
3. **Mission alignment first** — Prioritize problems directly tied to sustainable livelihoods
4. **Iterative deepening** — Start broad, then deepen understanding of the most impactful problems
5. **Cross-functional input** — Research, Product, and Engineering all contribute to problem discovery

---

## Phase 1: Foundation (Current)

**Target:** 25 validated problems

**Focus areas:**

- Career & Learning (highest priority for initial product)
- Freelancing & Business (core livelihood categories)
- Execution & Productivity (foundational to all categories)

**Timeline:** 8-12 weeks

**Activities:**

- [ ] Conduct 20+ exploratory interviews across target categories
- [ ] Deploy initial survey to validate top hypotheses
- [ ] Establish research operations (participant recruitment, scheduling, documentation)
- [ ] Populate Problem Repository with first 25 problems
- [ ] Score and prioritize all 25 problems
- [ ] Present findings to product team for feature alignment

**Success criteria:**

- 25 problems fully documented using Problem Template
- At least 10 validated through 2+ sources
- Coverage across 5+ categories
- Top 5 prioritized problems linked to product roadmap

---

## Phase 2: Expansion

**Target:** 50 validated problems

**Focus areas:**

- Finance & AI Adoption (emerging categories)
- Communication & Networking (social categories)
- Deepen understanding of top Phase 1 problems

**Timeline:** 8-12 weeks (after Phase 1)

**Activities:**

- [ ] Expand interview pool to include diverse demographics
- [ ] Deploy category-specific surveys
- [ ] Conduct expert interviews for complex domains
- [ ] Begin community research (Reddit, forums, social media)
- [ ] Add 25 new problems (total: 50)
- [ ] Re-score and re-prioritize all problems

**Success criteria:**

- 50 problems documented
- 25+ validated through 2+ sources
- Coverage across 10+ categories
- Evidence repository established with artifacts

---

## Phase 3: Depth

**Target:** 100 validated problems

**Focus areas:**

- Marketing & Sales (business growth categories)
- Mindset & Personal Growth (psychological categories)
- Leadership (organizational category)
- Deep-dive validation of highest-priority problems

**Timeline:** 12-16 weeks (after Phase 2)

**Activities:**

- [ ] Scale survey efforts (500+ responses per survey)
- [ ] Commission industry report analysis
- [ ] Conduct longitudinal studies on top problems
- [ ] Establish ongoing community monitoring
- [ ] Add 50 new problems (total: 100)
- [ ] Build problem relationship mapping (which problems connect)
- [ ] Begin cross-referencing problems with product features

**Success criteria:**

- 100 problems documented
- 50+ validated (at least 1 quantitative source)
- Coverage across all 15 categories
- Problem relationship map established
- Product features traceable to validated problems

---

## Phase 4: Mastery

**Target:** 200+ validated problems

**Focus areas:**

- Ongoing discovery across all categories
- International and cultural variations
- Emerging problems (AI-driven changes in work)
- Problem evolution tracking

**Timeline:** Ongoing (after Phase 3)

**Activities:**

- [ ] Establish automated problem discovery pipeline (AI-assisted)
- [ ] Continuous monitoring of industry trends and shifts
- [ ] Quarterly re-prioritization cycles
- [ ] Annual re-validation of all active problems
- [ ] Periodic research reports for leadership
- [ ] Open research publications (optional)

**Success criteria:**

- 200+ problems documented
- 100+ validated with high confidence
- Continuous discovery pipeline operational
- Problems updated within 30 days of significant changes
- Research insights directly inform quarterly product planning

## Quarterly Review Cadence

Every quarter, the research team will:

1. **Review progress** — Compare against roadmap targets
2. **Update priorities** — Adjust focus areas based on product needs
3. **Re-validate** — Check existing problems for continued relevance
4. **Publish findings** — Share insights with the broader organization
5. **Adjust roadmap** — Update timeline and activities as needed
