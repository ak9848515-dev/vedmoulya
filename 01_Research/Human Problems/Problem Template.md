## Usage Guidelines

1. **Copy the template** — Start each new problem by copying the above template.
2. **Fill in what you know** — Leave unknown fields blank rather than guessing.
3. **Update status** — Change the status field as the problem moves through validation.
4. **Link to evidence** — Always link to actual research artifacts, not just assertions.
5. **Cross-reference** — Link to related problems even if they're in different categories.
6. **Review quarterly** — During quarterly reviews, update any outdated information.
