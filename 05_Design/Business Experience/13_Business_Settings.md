# Business Settings

> **Document:** DES-008-D13 — Business Operating System Experience  
> **Status:** 🔒 **LOCKED** — Part of DES-008 Business Constitution v1.0

---

## Purpose

Business Settings allows users to control their business preferences, stages, integrations, and data management.

---

## Settings Sections

| Section          | Settings                                                |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Profile**      | Business stage, industry, solo/team/freelancer/startup  |
| **Preferences**  | Risk tolerance, notification frequency, review schedule |
| **Integrations** | Stripe, Calendly, Notion, Slack, email                  |
| **Documents**    | Business plan storage, legal docs, financial records    |
| **Privacy**      | Business data visibility, sharing controls              |
| **Data**         | Export all business data, reset business history        |
