# Business Insights

> **Document:** DES-008-D11 — Business Operating System Experience  
> **Status:** 🔒 **LOCKED** — Part of DES-008 Business Constitution v1.0

---

## Purpose

Business Insights provide personalized observations about the user's business journey — patterns, risks, opportunities, and progress — derived from their data, not generic advice.

---

## Insight Types

| Type                    | Example                                                            | Source                    |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------- |
| **Progress insight**    | "You've completed 12 customer interviews this month."              | Validation tracking       |
| **Pattern insight**     | "You tend to validate problems quickly but struggle with pricing." | Business pattern analysis |
| **Risk insight**        | "No customer conversations in 2 weeks — momentum risk."            | Activity gap detection    |
| **Opportunity insight** | "3 businesses in your network are hiring for what you offer."      | Network + market          |
| **Market insight**      | "Demand for AI screening tools is up 40% this quarter."            | Market data               |
| **Coach insight**       | "You've been avoiding the pricing assumption. Let's tackle it."    | AI Business Coach         |

---

## Weekly Business Review

| Section           | Content                                        | Duration |
| ----------------- | ---------------------------------------------- | -------- |
| **Progress**      | Validations, experiments, client conversations | 15s      |
| **Risks**         | Active risks, new risks detected               | 10s      |
| **Opportunity**   | New opportunities identified                   | 10s      |
| **Coach insight** | Most important observation                     | 20s      |
| **Next week**     | Recommended priorities                         | 15s      |
