## Preamble

This Constitution establishes the Business Experience for VedMoulya — a complete Business Operating System that helps users discover opportunities, validate ideas, plan, build, launch, operate, grow, measure, and evolve businesses throughout their entrepreneurial journey. This is NOT a CRM, NOT an ERP, NOT Notion, NOT ClickUp. Business is solving problems, creating value, serving people, building systems, earning trust, generating sustainable income, and continuous improvement.

**All specifications follow DES-001 v1.0 through DES-007 v1.0 exactly.**

## 4. Information Hierarchy (LOCKED)

```
P0 — ALWAYS VISIBLE:
  • Today's highest-impact action
  • Current business stage + priority
  • Risk indicator (if any active risk)

P1 — SHOWN BY DEFAULT:
  • Business Dashboard overview
  • Idea Studio (current idea status)
  • Active projects/experiments
  • AI Business Coach presence

P2 — CONTEXTUAL:
  • Detailed validation canvas
  • Business model details
  • Client pipeline
  • Financial summary
  • Business analytics

P3 — ON DEMAND:
  • Full business history
  • Business settings
  • Document archive
```

## 5. AI Business Coach Rules (LOCKED)

| Rule                             | Implementation                                              |
| -------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Never fabricates market data** | Distinguishes user evidence from market observations        |
| **No success promises**          | Never guarantees business outcomes                          |
| **Challenges assumptions**       | Plays devil's advocate respectfully                         |
| **Validation-first**             | Recommends validation before building                       |
| **Sources distinguished**        | Facts ≠ Assumptions ≠ Market observations ≠ Recommendations |

## 6. Success Metrics (LOCKED)

| Metric                 | Why                         | Not Vanity                         |
| ---------------------- | --------------------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| Validated problems     | Real need = real business   | Ideas are not validated            |
| Customer conversations | Evidence of market interest | Page views are not interest        |

| Products launched      | Value delivered             | Features built are not products    |
