# Responsive Experience

> **Document:** DES-008-D14 — Business Operating System Experience  
> **Status:** 🔒 **LOCKED** — Part of DES-008 Business Constitution v1.0

---

## Purpose

The Business Experience must feel native on every device — from mobile idea capture on the go to desktop analytics and business model planning.

---

## Device Layouts

| Device                | Business Dashboard              | Idea Studio                   | Business Coach     |
| --------------------- | ------------------------------- | ----------------------------- | ------------------ |
| **Mobile (<480px)**   | Single column, priority + radar | Full-screen idea card         | Full-screen chat   |
| **Tablet (768px)**    | 2-column: priority + progress   | Idea card + validation status | Overlay drawer     |
| **Desktop (1280px+)** | Full 4-zone layout              | Full idea canvas + coach      | Side-by-side coach |
