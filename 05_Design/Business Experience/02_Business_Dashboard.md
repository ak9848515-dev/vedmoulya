# Business Dashboard

> **Document:** DES-008-D02 — Business Operating System Experience  
> **Status:** 🔒 **LOCKED** — Part of DES-008 Business Constitution v1.0

---

## Purpose

The Business Dashboard shows the user's business home — today's priorities, current stage, opportunity radar, and highest-impact next action. It answers: "What matters most today? Which opportunity deserves attention? What should I do next?"
