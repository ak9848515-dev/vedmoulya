# Interview Experience

> **Document:** DES-006-D11 — Career Operating System Experience  
> **Status:** 🔒 **LOCKED** — Part of DES-006 Career Constitution v1.0

---

## Purpose

The Interview Experience helps users prepare for, practice, and learn from interviews — transforming high-stress events into growth opportunities.

---

## Interview Preparation

| Feature              | Description                                              |
| -------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Company research** | AI-compiled briefing on company, culture, recent news    |
| **Role analysis**    | Key requirements, typical questions, evaluation criteria |
| **Mock interviews**  | AI-conducted practice with real-time feedback            |
| **Question bank**    | Curated questions by role type, seniority, industry      |
| **Answer framework** | STAR method guidance, structure templates                |
| **Skill refresher**  | Quick review of relevant technical concepts              |
