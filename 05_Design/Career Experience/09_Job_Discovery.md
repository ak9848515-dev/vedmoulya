## Purpose

## AI Matching

| Factor              | Weight | Source                             |
| ------------------- | ------ | ---------------------------------- |
| Skill match         | 40%    | Skill Profile vs job requirements  |
| Experience level    | 25%    | Years, seniority, responsibilities |
| Industry preference | 15%    | User's target industries           |
| Location preference | 10%    | Remote/hybrid/onsite preference    |
| Company culture     | 10%    | User's stated values               |
