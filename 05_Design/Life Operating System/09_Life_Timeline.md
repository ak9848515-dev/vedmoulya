# Life Timeline

> **Document:** DES-010-D09 — Life Operating System Experience  
> **Status:** 🔒 **LOCKED** — Part of DES-010 Life OS Constitution v1.0

---

## Purpose

Life Timeline enables users to view their entire life journey through VedMoulya — from daily reflections through life chapters to major milestones and long-term vision — all in one continuous, meaningful timeline.

## Life Chapters Integration

Life Chapters (from DES-004 Memory & Knowledge) are the highest-level timeline structure. Each chapter represents a significant life phase:

| Chapter Source                     | Life OS Integration                                                   |
| ---------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Career chapters** (DES-006)      | Job changes, promotions, career transitions appear as chapter markers |
| **Learning chapters** (DES-007)    | Major skill acquisitions, certifications as chapter milestones        |
| **Business chapters** (DES-008)    | Business launches, pivots, exits as chapter boundaries                |
| **Marketplace chapters** (DES-009) | Major collaborations, trust milestones as chapter events              |
| **Personal chapters** (DES-004)    | Moves, relationships, personal growth as chapter themes               |

## Quality Review

| Dimension                         | Assessment                                                                                                          |
| --------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Why**                           | Life Timeline gives meaning to daily actions — without it, users see tasks, not progress                            |
| **Life Psychology Reasoning**     | Narrative identity — humans construct identity through life stories; timeline makes progress visible and meaningful |
| **Human-Centered Reasoning**      | People want to see how far they've come — not just what's next                                                      |
| **Accessibility Impact**          | Timeline is navigable by keyboard; each entry screen reader accessible; alternative text for all visuals            |
| **Trust Impact**                  | Honest timeline (including setbacks) builds deeper trust than selective positive framing                            |
| **Consistency with DES Missions** | Integrates DES-004 Life Chapters with all other module timelines                                                    |
| **Implementation Complexity**     | Medium-High — requires cross-module timeline aggregation, milestone detection, visualization                        |
| **Future Scalability**            | Can add predictive timeline (AI-projected trajectory), shared timeline moments                                      |
