## Preamble

People do not live in modules. People live one life. Career influences learning. Learning influences business. Business influences finance. Health influences execution. Knowledge influences decisions. Everything is connected.

This Constitution establishes the Life Operating System for VedMoulya — the integration layer that makes every previous mission (DES-001 through DES-009) work together as ONE continuous life experience. The user should never feel they are switching modules. They should feel they are progressing through life.

**DES-010 does not replace previous missions. It is the integration layer that connects them.**

## 3. Integration Principles (LOCKED)

1. **One life, one OS** — Users experience one continuous life, not separate modules
2. **Natural transitions** — Context switches feel like life progression, not app switching
3. **Adaptive presence** — The OS adapts to time, energy, focus, life stage — never intrusive
4. **Memory-native** — Every interaction is aware of user's history, context, and trajectory
5. **AI as companion, not interface** — The Life Companion connects modules; it doesn't dominate
6. **Silence is strategy** — The best action is often no action; the OS respects attention
7. **Progress over completion** — Life is a journey, not a checklist; celebrate direction, not just destination
8. **User sovereignty** — Every adaptation is transparent and controllable; user always decides

## 5. Daily Life Flow (LOCKED)

The Life OS follows a natural daily rhythm that adapts to the user's life, not the other way around:

```
MORNING (5:00 - 11:59)
  ├── Wake & Morning Welcome (DES-003)
  ├── Daily Brief (Life OS)
  │   ├── Today's Focus (DES-003)
  │   ├── Learning Moment (DES-007) — if relevant
  │   ├── Career Opportunity (DES-006) — if relevant
  │   └── Business/Marketplace Check (DES-008/009) — if relevant
  └── AI Life Companion — proactive suggestions only

AFTERNOON (12:00 - 16:59)
  ├── Execution mode (DES-003)
  ├── Deep work blocks
  ├── Learning sessions (DES-007)
  ├── Meeting/Collaboration time
  └── AI Life Companion — responsive, checks progress

EVENING (17:00 - 20:59)
  ├── Reflection (Life OS)
  ├── Knowledge update (DES-004)
  ├── Memory capture (DES-004)
  ├── Decision review
  └── AI Life Companion — silent, waits for user

NIGHT (21:00 - 4:59)
  ├── Light planning (DES-003)
  ├── Tomorrow's preview
  └── AI Life Companion — silent, responds only
```

## 6. Life States (LOCKED)

The Life OS recognizes and adapts to the user's current life state:

| Life State      | Energy      | Focus       | AI Behavior            | Content Priority                |
| --------------- | ----------- | ----------- | ---------------------- | ------------------------------- |
| **Focus**       | High        | Single task | Minimal — protect flow | Execution > Learning            |
| **Exploration** | Medium      | Broad       | Suggest — inspire      | Discovery > Career > Learning   |
| **Learning**    | Medium-High | Growth      | Teach — Socratic       | Learning > Practice > Apply     |
| **Execution**   | High        | Multiple    | Track — check progress | Career > Business > Marketplace |
| **Reflection**  | Low         | Past        | Listen — ask gently    | Memory > Knowledge > Review     |
| **Recovery**    | Very Low    | Rest        | Silent — only respond  | Minimal — only essentials       |
| **Transition**  | Variable    | Change      | Guide — support        | Career > Learning > Planning    |
| **Celebration** | High        | Achievement | Acknowledge — quiet    | Milestone > Growth > Share      |

## 7. Information Hierarchy (LOCKED)

```
P0 — ALWAYS VISIBLE:
  • Time-aware greeting & atmosphere
  • Today's Focus / Current Priority
  • Life Score (trend direction)

P1 — SHOWN BY DEFAULT:
  • AI Life Companion presence (silent when appropriate)
  • Current life state indicator
  • Cross-module context bar (what's relevant now)
  • Active life journey progress

P2 — CONTEXTUAL:
  • Module-specific content (learning, career, business, marketplace)
  • Memory moments
  • Recommendations (max 2)
  • Reflection prompts (evening)

P3 — ON DEMAND:
  • Full module views
  • History & archives
  • Settings
```
