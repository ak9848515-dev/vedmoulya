## Recommendations for DES-011

Based on the Life OS architecture, the next mission (DES-011) should be:

### Recommended: **Finance & Wealth OS Experience**

**Rationale:**

- Finance is the most significant missing life domain after Career, Learning, Business, and Marketplace
- Financial data is tightly coupled to Career (income), Business (revenue), and Marketplace (earnings)
- Users need financial context to make informed life decisions
- The Life OS integration layer (DES-010) provides the perfect foundation for financial integration

**Key Integration Points with Life OS:**

- Career → Income tracking, financial goal setting
- Business → Revenue management, business finance
- Marketplace → Payment processing, earnings analytics
- Learning → ROI of learning investments
- Execution → Financial task prioritization
- Life Timeline → Net worth trajectory, financial milestones
