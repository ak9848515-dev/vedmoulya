## Preamble

This document is the definitive experience governance document for VedMoulya. It is NOT another module. It is NOT another feature. It becomes the highest UX authority for the entire product.

Every future screen, every component, every AI interaction, every animation, every experience must follow this document.

**This document supersedes individual DES mission styling rules while respecting their functional specifications.**

Where a previous mission defines a **functional** requirement, that requirement stands. Where a previous mission defines a **styling** or **experience** rule that conflicts with this Bible, this Bible takes precedence.

---

## Design Language

Everything should reflect:

- **Calm Intelligence** — Thoughtful, unhurried, wise
- **Quiet Confidence** — Doesn't need to shout; knows its value
- **Premium Simplicity** — Simple because it's refined, not because it's unfinished
- **Professional Trust** — Worthy of the user's most important life decisions
- **Human Warmth** — Technology that feels human, not corporate
- **Explainable AI** — Every AI output is transparent and attributable
- **Execution First** — Help users do, not just know

**No visual noise. No unnecessary decoration. No gamification. No manipulative UX.**

## Experience Governance Model

| Authority                               | Scope                            | Approval Required |
| --------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- | ----------------- |
| **Experience Bible**                    | Global UX decisions              | CDO + CXO + CEO   |
| **DES Missions**                        | Module-specific functional specs | CPO + CDO         |
| **Design Reviews**                      | Cross-cutting experience changes | CDO               |
| **DCA (Design Constitution Amendment)** | Changes to this Bible            | CDO + CXO + CEO   |

### Hierarchy of Authority

```
EXPERIENCE BIBLE (D00-D15)
    ↑
DES-001 through DES-010 (functional specifications)
    ↑
DES-010A D01-D15 (detailed specs per domain)
```

Any conflict between a lower-tier document and a higher-tier document is resolved in favor of the higher-tier document.
