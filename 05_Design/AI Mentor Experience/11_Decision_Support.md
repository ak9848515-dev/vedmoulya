## Purpose

Decision Support helps users make better decisions by clarifying options, identifying criteria, evaluating trade-offs, and building decision confidence. The Decision Partner mode is analytical, neutral, and structured.

---

## Decision Support Flow

```
1. FRAME — Define the decision
   "What decision are you facing? What makes it complex?"

2. OPTIONS — Identify and list alternatives
   "What are your options? Let's list them all, even the unconventional ones."

3. CRITERIA — What matters most?
   "What factors are most important in this decision? Let's weight them."

4. EVALUATE — Assess each option
   Decision matrix with weighted criteria + pros/cons per option

5. REFLECT — Gut check
   "Given what the analysis shows, how does each option feel?"

6. DECIDE — Reach conclusion
   "Which option aligns most with your values and goals?"

7. FOLLOW-UP — Track outcome
   "I'll remember this decision. Let's check back in 30 days."
```
