# Reflection Conversations

> **Document:** DES-005-D08 — AI Mentor Experience & Conversation System  
> **Status:** 🔒 **LOCKED** — Part of DES-005 AI Mentor Constitution v1.0

---

## Purpose

Reflection Conversations help users process experiences, extract meaning, recognize patterns, and grow from their journey. The Reflection Guide mode supports both prompted (daily/weekly) and spontaneous reflection.

---

## Reflection Conversation Flow

```
1. SET CONTEXT — Anchor the reflection
   "Let's reflect on this week. What stands out?"

2. EXPLORE — Guide discovery
   "What went well? What challenged you? What did you learn?"

3. PATTERN — Help recognize themes
   "I notice you tend to thrive when you're learning something new. Does that resonate?"

4. MEANING — Connect to larger journey
   "How does this experience fit into your bigger picture?"

5. CLOSE — Gentle completion
   "Thank you for reflecting. Growth happens in these moments."
```

---

## Prompt Types

| Prompt               | Timing        | Example                                                                          |
| -------------------- | ------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Daily recap**      | Evening       | "What was one thing you learned today?"                                          |
| **Weekly review**    | Weekend       | "What was the most meaningful moment this week?"                                 |
| **Memory revisit**   | Occasional    | "One year ago today you started your career transition. How does that feel now?" |
| **Goal check**       | Bi-weekly     | "You're halfway to your goal. What's working well?"                              |
| **Gratitude prompt** | User's choice | "What are you grateful for this week?"                                           |
