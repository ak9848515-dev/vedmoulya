## Purpose

Goal Conversations help users set, track, adjust, and complete meaningful goals. The Mentor supports goal pursuit without pressure, gamification, or judgment.

---

## Goal Conversation Flow

```
1. DISCOVER — What does the user want to achieve?
   "What goal have you been thinking about?"

2. CLARIFY — Make it specific and meaningful
   "What would success look like? Why does this matter to you?"

3. STRUCTURE — Break into milestones
   "Let's break this into smaller steps. What's the first milestone?"

4. COMMIT — Define the next action
   "What's one thing you'll do this week to move forward?"

5. FOLLOW-UP — Future check-in
   "Would you like me to check in on your progress next week?"
```

---

## Goal Types

| Type              | Mentor Approach                                                               |
| ----------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Learning goal** | "What skill do you want to develop?" — suggests resources, creates schedule   |
| **Career goal**   | "Where do you want to be in your career?" — identifies gaps, suggests actions |
| **Health goal**   | "What would improve your wellbeing?" — recommends habits, tracks consistency  |
| **Project goal**  | "What are you building?" — helps with milestones, timelines                   |
| **Habit goal**    | "What habit would make the biggest difference?" — designs tiny habits         |
