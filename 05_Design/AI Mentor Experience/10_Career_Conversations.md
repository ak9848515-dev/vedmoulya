> **Document:** DES-005-D10 — AI Mentor Experience & Conversation System

---

## Purpose

Career Conversations help users navigate their professional journey — from skill development to role transitions to long-term career strategy.

---

## Career Conversation Flow

```
1. ORIENT — Understand current state
   "How is your current role going? What's energizing you? What's draining you?"

2. VISION — Explore aspirations
   "Where do you see your career in 2-3 years? What kind of impact do you want to make?"

3. GAP — Identify development areas
   "Here's what you have. Here's what your target role needs. Let's build a bridge."

4. PLAN — Create actionable roadmap
   "Let's create a 90-day plan with specific skills to develop, people to connect with."

5. EXECUTE — Take next step
   "What's one action you'll take this week?"
```

## Career Conversation Topics

| Topic                     | Mentor Approach                                                |
| ------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Role transition**       | Skills gap analysis, networking strategy, application guidance |
| **Skill development**     | Course recommendations, project ideas, practice opportunities  |
| **Leadership growth**     | Management skills, team dynamics, influence without authority  |
| **Career satisfaction**   | Values alignment, work-life integration, purpose connection    |
| **Interview preparation** | Practice conversations, STAR framework, company research       |
