## Description

A comprehensive glossary of terms, acronyms, and concepts used across the VedMoulya project. This document ensures consistent language across all teams and documentation.

---

## Terms

### A

- **Agent:** An autonomous AI entity that performs tasks within the platform (Coach, Planner, Analyst, Mentor).
- **Architecture Decision Record (ADR):** A document capturing an important architectural decision with context and rationale.

### C

- **CMP:** Company/Competitive sprint type — focuses on market positioning and competitive analysis.
- **Context Engine:** AI subsystem that aggregates and prioritizes contextual information for each interaction.

### D

- **Decision Engine:** AI subsystem that evaluates options and makes context-aware decisions.
- **Design Token:** A named value that represents a design attribute (color, spacing, typography).

### E

- **Execution Engine:** AI subsystem that orchestrates the reliable execution of plans and tasks.
- **ERD:** Entity-Relationship Diagram — visual representation of data entities and their relationships.

### H

- **HPI (Human Progress Index):** A metrics framework that measures user growth across skills, career, financial, and personal dimensions.
- **Human Execution OS:** The conceptual operating system that transforms knowledge into sustainable livelihoods.

### K

- **Knowledge Graph:** A dynamic graph database representing relationships between concepts, skills, and opportunities.

### M

- **Memory:** AI subsystem for storing and retrieving persistent context across user sessions.

### O

- **Orchestrator:** Central coordinator that routes AI requests through appropriate subsystems.

### P

- **Persona:** A representative user profile used for design and development decisions.
- **Planning Engine:** AI subsystem that generates and optimizes actionable plans from user goals.
- **Prompt Engine:** AI subsystem that manages prompt templates, assembly, and optimization.
- **PRD:** Product Requirements Document — detailed specifications for a product or feature.

### R

- **Reasoning Engine:** AI subsystem that provides structured logical reasoning capabilities.

### S

- **Sprint Type:** Classification of a sprint by focus area (CMP, PRD, ARC, AIE, ENG, MKT, OPS).

### V

- **VedMoulya:** The company and platform name, representing the mission of empowering livelihood through knowledge and execution.
