## Mission

Empower every determined individual to build a sustainable livelihood through knowledge, execution, and intelligent technology.

## Vision

Create the world's most trusted execution platform that transforms knowledge into livelihoods. Help one million people create sustainable livelihoods.

## Engineering Principles

Per the Constitution (`03_Architecture/VEDMOULYA_CONSTITUTION.md`):

1. Reuse mature open-source software whenever appropriate.
2. Build only capabilities that differentiate VedMoulya.
3. Every external technology must be wrapped behind VedMoulya interfaces.
4. Every AI call must have a token budget.
5. Every AI call must have a cost budget.
6. Every AI call must have a quality target.
7. Every AI call must use the minimum necessary context.
8. Business modules must never directly call AI providers.
9. All AI execution must go through the Enterprise Intelligence layer.
10. Every sprint must include documentation.
11. Every architectural decision must be documented.
12. Revenue before perfection.
