## Description

Sprint type focused on user research, market analysis, technology exploration, and data-driven decision making.

## Purpose

Conduct systematic research to inform product decisions, validate hypotheses, and discover new opportunities for the VedMoulya platform.

## Scope

- User research (interviews, surveys, diary studies)
- Market research and competitive analysis
- Technology evaluation and proof-of-concept
- Data analysis and insight generation
- Hypothesis testing and experiment design
- Literature and academic research reviews
- Research synthesis and reporting

## Responsibilities

- Plan and execute research studies
- Synthesize research findings into actionable insights
- Share research results with cross-functional teams
- Maintain a research repository for future reference
- Recommend data-informed product decisions
- Track research impact on product outcomes
