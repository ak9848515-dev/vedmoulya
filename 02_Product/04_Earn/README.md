## Description

The Earn module enables users to monetize their skills, knowledge, and creations through services, products, and opportunities.

---

## Purpose

Help users generate sustainable income by connecting their skills and work with paying opportunities.

## Responsibilities

- Facilitate service listing and client acquisition
- Manage payments and financial transactions
- Track earnings and financial growth
- Support pricing and value communication
- Integrate with marketplace and business tools
