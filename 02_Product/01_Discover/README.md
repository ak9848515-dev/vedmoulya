## Description

## Purpose

Help users discover what's possible — surfacing opportunities, career paths, learning resources, and connections that match their aspirations and capabilities.

## Responsibilities

- Provide personalized recommendations for skills, careers, and opportunities
- Enable exploration of the knowledge graph and skill universe
- Surface relevant learning content and resources
- Connect users with mentors, peers, and communities
