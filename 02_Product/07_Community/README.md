## Description

The Community module fosters connection, collaboration, and support among VedMoulya users through groups, forums, events, and peer interactions.

---

## Purpose

Build a thriving community where users support each other, share knowledge, collaborate on projects, and celebrate achievements together.

## Responsibilities

- Enable community groups and forums
- Facilitate peer-to-peer collaboration
- Host events and workshops
- Recognize and celebrate user achievements
- Moderate and ensure safe community space
