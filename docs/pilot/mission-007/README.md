## Disclosures this kit enforces in every conversation

The following six points appear in the discovery script, the drafts and the
readiness checklist. They are not optional phrasing — they are the difference
between an honest offer and a misleading one.

1. **Article writing and editing need a human.** AI may draft only if a live
   provider is separately verified and the client approves the routing. No
   AI-generated paragraph is delivered unreviewed.
2. **Payment collection is not integrated.** The product records and verifies;
   money moves outside it.
3. **A recorded payment is not proof of receipt.** It becomes "received" only after
   a named human compares the reference against the real account.
4. **Sending and publishing are manual.** Nothing is emailed, posted or scheduled
   by the product.
5. **Export is Markdown and HTML only.** No PDF, no DOCX — the service returns
   `supported: false` for those.
6. **There is no hosted production service.** No account, no SLA, no uptime
   commitment, no multi-tenant deployment.

## The ladder this kit refuses to skip

```
prospect ≠ conversation ≠ qualification ≠ interest ≠ acceptance
        ≠ payment ≠ verified revenue ≠ delivered
```

Each arrow needs evidence, listed in [`../OUTCOME-TRACKING.md`](../OUTCOME-TRACKING.md) §1.
The weekly review records which rung you are actually on, not which one you feel on.

## How to use the kit, in order

1. **Fill slots 1–3 in the tracker** with named people you may lawfully contact.
2. **Draft one message** from `OUTREACH-DRAFTS.md`, replacing every bracket.
3. **Get owner approval** — the six questions in `../ACQUISITION-WORKFLOW.md` §0.
4. **Send it by hand.** Log it. Nothing sends itself.
5. **If they reply, run the 15-minute script.** Record their words, not your summary.
6. **Log reactions** in `PRICE-VALIDATION.md` §4 and any objection in `OBJECTION-LOG.md`.
7. **Only if qualified**, prepare a proposal from `../PROPOSAL-AND-SOW.md`.
8. **Before accepting**, run `PILOT-READINESS-CHECKLIST.md` end to end.
9. **Every week**, fill one block of `WEEKLY-REVIEW.md`.

## What would have to be true before this kit can be called successful

- Ten slots have real names and lawful bases — **or fewer, honestly**.
- Three discovery conversations have happened.
- Price reactions are recorded, including the uncomfortable ones.
- At least one proposal is in front of the owner for approval.
- **A deposit has been received and verified** — and only then: revenue.

Until the last line happens, the honest answer to "has the price been validated?"
is **no**.
