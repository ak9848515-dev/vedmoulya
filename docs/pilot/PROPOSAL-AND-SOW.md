# Pilot proposal and statement of work

**Template.** Everything in `[square brackets]` is a fact only the operator can
supply. Replace every bracket, delete the italic guidance lines, and get the owner's
approval (see [`ACQUISITION-WORKFLOW.md`](./ACQUISITION-WORKFLOW.md) §0) before
sending. Nothing here has been sent to anyone.

> Do **not** use [`../business/PROPOSAL_TEMPLATE.md`](../business/PROPOSAL_TEMPLATE.md)
> for this offer: it quotes USD 4,400 for a monthly retainer that has never been
> delivered. See [`OFFER-COMPARISON.md`](./OFFER-COMPARISON.md).

```text
PILOT PROPOSAL & STATEMENT OF WORK

Offer:        Revenue Integrity Pilot (three sessions)
Proposal #:   [PROP-YYYY-###]
Prepared for: [Client company] — [Contact name, role]
Prepared by:  [Operator name] — [email] — [phone]
Date:         [YYYY-MM-DD]        Valid until: [date + 14 days]
Price status: PROPOSAL UNDER VALIDATION — not a published rate

────────────────────
1. WHAT YOU TOLD ME
   [Two or three sentences in their words, including their quantified
   pain. Quote them. Do not summarise into marketing language.]

2. THE OUTCOME YOU ARE BUYING
   By Day 14 you will be able to answer "how much did I actually get paid
   this month?" from one place, and prove any figure on that list against
   a receipt reference — with a ledger that cannot double-count a retried
   entry.

3. SCOPE — WHAT IS DELIVERED
   S1  Setup: one client, one brand profile, one project in your workspace.
   S2  One article, 600–1,200 words, human-authored, edited, reviewed and
       approved by you; exported as Markdown (.md) and HTML (.html).
   S3  One invoice raised in the system with the amount, currency and due
       date you specify; marked sent.
   S4  One payment recorded with the exact bank/processor reference,
       reviewed and verified by a named human.
   S5  Live demonstration that the same submission with the same
       idempotency key produces one ledger row, not two.
   S6  A written operating one-pager for month 2, agreed with you in S3.
   S7  Written summary after each session; 30 days of email support.

4. SCHEDULE
   Day -3   Scope confirmed in writing; readiness checks pass; dates held.
   Day 0    Session 1 (2h) — S1, S2, S3.
   Day 1    Files delivered; you confirm the numbers.
   Day 7    Session 2 (2h) — S4, S5.   [Deferred if no payment has arrived.]
   Day 14   Session 3 (2h) — you run the loop unaided; S6 signed off.
   Day 15   Review call (30 min) — what worked, what did not, next step.
   Day 44   Support window closes.

   If a session slips, the dates move. Any change to scope, dates or the
   deliverables requires a written amendment to this document.

5. EXCLUSIONS
   - Publishing, scheduling or distribution anywhere.
   - Collecting money from your customers; card processing; tax advice.
   - More than one article; ongoing monthly production.
   - PDF or Word (.docx) files — Markdown and HTML only.
   - Hosted account, SLA, uptime guarantee, 24/7 support.
   - Content strategy, brand identity, SEO audits, competitor research.
   - Data migration beyond one client and one invoice.
   - Legal review of the contract.

6. YOUR RESPONSIBILITIES
   - [Named owner] attends all three sessions and can decide in the room.
   - One client, one brief (≤ 500 words), real invoice details (amount,
     currency, due date) by Day 0.
   - The real payment reference once money arrives — never an invented one.
   - Sign-in to your own workspace; a channel to receive files.
   - Brand guidelines you want honoured.

   We will not ask for bank logins, email/social passwords, card numbers,
   or your customers' personal data.

7. PRICE AND PAYMENT — PROPOSAL
   Total: USD [1,500]
     - USD [500] on written acceptance (books the dates).
     - USD [1,000] when every acceptance criterion in §8 is met.
   This price is a proposal for this pilot. It is not a published rate and
   is open to discussion before acceptance; once accepted in writing it is
   the agreed price for this scope.

   Payment is by [bank transfer / whatever the operator accepts]. The
   product records and verifies a payment; it does not collect one.

8. ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA — checked together on Day 14
   [ ] Your client record and one invoice exist with the amount you stated.
   [ ] One article was captured, reviewed, approved and exported; the file
       opens and reads correctly.
   [ ] One payment row has a real, independently sourced reference and was
       promoted recorded → verified by a named reviewer.
   [ ] The revenue view never showed an unreviewed payment as received
       revenue (shown live).
   [ ] A duplicate submission with the same key produced one row (shown live).
   [ ] You completed the month-2 loop unaided within 30 minutes.
   [ ] The operating one-pager was accepted by you in writing.

   Unmet criteria are recorded as unmet. The balance is not invoiced until
   all seven are met or the parties agree in writing to a reduced scope.

9. REVISIONS AND CANCELLATION
   See the revision and cancellation terms in this pack (§9 of
   DELIVERY-TERMS.md). Summary:
   - One revision round on the article is included; further rounds are out
     of scope and would need a new agreement.
   - The deposit is refundable in full until 48 hours before Session 1.
   - After Session 1 the deposit is non-refundable (work has begun).
   - Either party may cancel in writing; work stops at the next session
     boundary, and nothing is owed for work not yet delivered.

10. DATA HANDLING AND APPROVALS
    Governed by CLIENT-DATA-HANDLING.md. In short: your article text may
    be sent to the AI provider only if we agree to use AI drafting — and
    you will be told which provider and model before the first draft. No
    card data, no customer personal data. Payment rows are append-only and
    cannot be deleted; demo entries stay in a throwaway workspace.

11. WHAT COULD GO WRONG, AND WHAT WE DO
    - A session slips → dates move; price does not.
    - The article needs a rewrite → one additional revision round included.
    - No real payment has arrived by Day 7 → Session 2 uses a clearly
      labelled sample; the "real payment" item stays open and the balance
      is not due until it closes.
    - Your AI provider is unavailable → the article is written without it;
      no AI-generated text is delivered unreviewed.

12. TO ACCEPT
    Reply "accepted" to this email. I will send the deposit invoice and the
    three calendar holds. Acceptance makes §3, §5, §7 and §9 binding for
    this pilot.

    [Operator name] _____________________  Date: ____________
    [Client name]   _____________________  Date: ____________
```

## How to use this document

1. Fill §1 from the discovery call notes — their words, not yours.
2. Confirm §3 matches what the product actually does. Do not add a deliverable
   because the prospect asked for it; use §9/§11 or decline.
3. Send the pilot package's acceptance criteria (§8) unchanged — they are the same
   seven checked in [`ACQUISITION-WORKFLOW.md`](./ACQUISITION-WORKFLOW.md) §6.
4. Keep a copy in the proposal module and log the send as an interaction.
   The product does not email anything.
