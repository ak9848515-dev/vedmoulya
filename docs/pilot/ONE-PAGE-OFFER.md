## The problem this addresses

If you bill several clients a month, "how much did I actually get paid this
month?" is harder to answer than it should be. The invoice lives in one tool, the
payment lands in a bank app or a processor, and the confirmation lives in a
message thread. Money you _think_ arrived is not money you have _checked_.

This pilot installs one habit: **the number does not move until a human checks the
receipt** — and the ledger cannot double-count a retried entry.

## How the money is verified (important)

The product **records and verifies** a payment. It does **not** collect one.
Invoicing you and receiving the transfer happen outside the product. When the
transfer arrives, I record it with the exact reference from your bank or
processor, and only a named human who has compared that reference against the
source account can mark it **verified**. Until then it is "recorded, awaiting
verification" — it is not revenue, and it will not be reported as such.
