## In-app lifecycle

`draft → active (on approval) → expired / terminated`

- Approving moves the contract to **active** and enables **expiry tracking**.
- **Renew** bumps the version and extends the dates (renewal flag set).
- Contracts expiring within 30/60 days raise **notifications** and appear in the
  Operations hub "Attention" panel.
