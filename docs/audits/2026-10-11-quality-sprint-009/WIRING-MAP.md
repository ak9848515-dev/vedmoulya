## Pre-implementation acceptance criteria

1. Launch reads an owner-scoped persisted opportunity; missing or cross-owner IDs create no Mission.
2. Launch requires a persisted approval record and runs the existing suitability assessor immediately before claiming launch; HIGH risk in either the persisted source assessment or current canonical assessor blocks Mission creation. The advisory suitability score is recorded with the launch claim; an explicit human approval remains the authorization boundary for non-HIGH results.
3. Only the authenticated explicit launch action or an already-authorized policy can invoke the launch procedure; qualification or approval alone does not auto-start work.
4. Repeated and concurrent launch calls create at most one canonical Mission while process/association evidence remains available and return its durable association or an honest in-progress/failure response. Crash-safe exactly-once recovery across the Mission-created/link-not-persisted window is unresolved.
5. The durable association preserves owner, opportunity, and Mission IDs and is queryable from either side.
6. Mission work enters through the existing `createAndRun` and canonical Mission lifecycle with a bounded objective derived from recorded opportunity facts.
7. Mission failure, blocked state, missing verification, or persistence failure is never reported as successful opportunity execution.
8. Proposal/delivery content remains a draft until the required approval; this sprint performs no external proposal, message, purchase, or submission.
9. Existing audit/lifecycle records distinguish approval, launch claim, association, Mission outcome, and failure to the extent supported by current contracts; no redundant status model is added.
10. Interrupted recovery rechecks owner, persisted approval, risk/suitability, and idempotency before resuming. The current implementation does this for `PLANNED` claims, but cannot prove whether an unlinked Mission was created before a crash; durable reconciliation remains BLOCKED pending canonical Mission idempotency or a durable launch-intent record.

## Smallest safe path

Audit the existing launch guard and recovery tests first. Add only the narrow missing gate or traceability behavior at the current API/port seam, reusing the existing opportunity lifecycle and Mission services. Do not modify the frozen roadmap or activate orchestration-fabric authority.
